TF Lens is an interactive, browser-based viewer designed for exploring, analyzing, and troubleshooting Terraform plans and state JSON files. It parses plan/state outputs entirely in the browser, showing resources, modules, outputs, and variables, with warnings for unknown/malformed resources and a sleek HashiCorp-inspired design.
