It renders a glossy, physically-lit cube in the browser that either scrambles and solves itself, or reads a physical cube face-by-face through your camera, classifies all 54 stickers by color, and animates the  full solution. I originally built it because [my son's friend is a speed cuber](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/-nyUyGbuTAc) and I wanted to make something fun for them. After I shared the visualizer on Reddit, someone  nerd-sniped me into taking it further - ["_cool, but can it actually scan and solve my cube_?"](https://www.reddit.com/r/coolgithubprojects/comments/1u2qu2o/comment/oqzstpm/).... so I built the webcam scanner and solver 🤯 

It also has an optional GitHub contribution graph-type color theme, just because.
