Generate a custom GitHub avatar with your favorite World Cup 2026 team's colors! Enter your GitHub username, pick a team, and download a PNG with a team-colored ribbon along the lower edge and a country code. Like [LinkedIn's `#OPENTOWORK`](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a507508/let-recruiters-know-you-re-open-to-work?lang=en), but instead of signaling career availability, you’re signaling that you are emotionally unavailable for the next 90 minutes plus stoppage time.
