dailyspark is a simple daily companion that combines a calendar view, an inspiring quote of the day and an optional song recommendation. 
It was built to bring organization an inspiration together in one place, helping users start their day with intention rather than just checking a schedule. 
The combination of thoughtful quotes, music and a clean daily overview creates a positve and uplifting  experience every morning.
