AI Engineer Coach is a VS Code extension that reads your local AI session logs and turns them into actionable insights without sending data off your machine. Track practice scores and trends, detect 45 anti-patterns across prompt quality and context management, and measure AI-generated code output by language, model, and harness. It also helps you discover reusable skills from repeated prompts and score the agentic readiness of your workspaces.
