AzImg CLI is a simple command-line tool for creating and editing images with Azure OpenAI. I built it because I wanted a reliable way to turn prompts into image files from scripts, agents, and CI workflows without having to click through a UI. It takes care of the practical details like profiles, output folders, filenames, manifests, and checksums, so the whole workflow feels clean, repeatable, and easy to trust.
