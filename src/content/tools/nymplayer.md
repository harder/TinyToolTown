I wanted to spin my vinyl records on a USB-equipped turntable and listen straight through my computer speakers or headphones. Sure, there are dozens of ways to do this, but I didn't want the hassle of heavy audio software. I was looking for a lightning-fast, zero-fuss setup—completely skipping all the complicated bells and whistles you get with tools like VLC or Audacity!
