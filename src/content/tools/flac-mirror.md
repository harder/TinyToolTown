This tool is for syncing music that you own to a target e.g. an SD card, so you can take your music with you. It converts FLAC files to MP3 and then copies them to the target. A target is kept in sync with your FLAC files source, handling additions and deletions. I built this as I have returned to the delights of compact disc and want to take my music with me in my car.
