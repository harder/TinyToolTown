Phil’s Time is a simple time zone comparison app for planning across cities. Add the places you care about, choose a date and time, and see the matching local time in each city. You can reorder cities, scrub time directly from any city, and share a link that preserves the exact comparison.

Features

* Compare times across cities around the world.
* Search by city, country, state, time zone, or aliases.
* Use the first city as the anchor time for the comparison.
* Pick a precise date and time, or jump back to now.
* See each city’s local time, date, UTC offset, and short time zone label.
* Supports offline use.
