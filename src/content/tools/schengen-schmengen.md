Schengen Zone visa rules can get complicated when you come and go as a retiree with lots of time on your hands for slow travel. This is how my parents found themselves after retirement. They needed a tool to help them figure out how to travel through Europe on a budget and within the rules of the Schengen Zone visa. This was a perfect opportunity for me to vibe a tool for them and anyone else who's coming and going to Schengen Zone countries multiple times within a 6 months period.
