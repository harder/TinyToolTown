A Manifest V3 browser extension for Edge & Chrome. When a new AI model launches and your LinkedIn feed fills with 'AI expert' takes, NoAiSlop covers every post that mentions your keywords with a translucent, dismissible "NO AI SLOP" stamp. The post stays put — peek through it or close the stamp — and blocking auto-expires on a date you choose, so it switches itself off after the hype. Runs entirely on your device: no servers, no analytics, no tracking.
