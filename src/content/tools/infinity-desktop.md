Infinity Desktop turns your Windows desktop into one large scrollable workspace for your open windows.

Instead of constantly Alt-Tabbing, digging through the taskbar, or jumping between disconnected virtual desktops, you can hold a modifier key and scroll sideways through your workspace. Your windows stay laid out visually, so it feels more like moving around one extended desktop than switching between separate places.

It also includes live window previews and quick search/filtering, making it easy to find the window you need without breaking your flow. I built it because Windows gets messy fast when you have lots open, and I wanted a smoother, faster, more visual way to move around the desktop.
